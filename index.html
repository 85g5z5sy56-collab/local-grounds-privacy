Local Grounds Privacy Policy Last updated: 12 October 2026 Who we are Local Grounds is a personal journal and rating app for independent coffee shops and bakeries, starting in Norwich, UK. It is run by Jonathan Clark, who is responsible for your personal data (the data controller). You can contact us at jonclark97@hotmail.com. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who can see it, and how you can delete it. What we collect We collect only what the app needs to work: Account details: your email address, a password (stored securely by our sign-in provider, so we never see it), a username and a display name. A profile photo is optional. Your reviews: the venue, the date of your visit, each coffee or bakery item with its score out of 10, and optional details you add (price, notes, beans or roaster, milk, photos, and atmosphere, service and value ratings). Each review also records who can see it. Your activity: friend requests and friendships, people you block, venues you save, favourite bakery items, and reports or venue suggestions you send us. Technical data: our hosting provider may keep standard security logs, such as IP address and device type. The app has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell personal data. How we use it We use your information to run the app: to sign you in, keep your reviews and saved places, work out rankings, show your friends' recommendations, and keep the community safe by handling reports and blocks. We do not use your data for advertising. Under UK data protection law, we rely on these legal bases: providing the service you asked for (your account and reviews), our legitimate interest in keeping the app safe and free of abuse (moderation and security), and your consent where you grant a phone permission such as location or photos. Who can see your reviews and photos You choose who sees each review. New reviews start as Private, unless you change your default in your profile. Private: only you. Friends: you and people whose friend request you have accepted. Public: other signed-in users, who will see your username and display name beside the review. Public reviews also count towards community rankings. Private and friends-only reviews are never included in community rankings. Friend requests need your acceptance. You can block someone, which removes any friendship and hides your reviews from each other. You can change a review's visibility, edit it or delete it at any time. Photos follow the same visibility as the review they are attached to. Your profile photo, if you add one, is stored in a public area, so anyone with its link can open it. Location, photos and permissions Location: the app asks for your location only when you tap the button to sort venues by distance. Your position is used on your phone to work out distances. It is not sent to our servers or stored. You can refuse, and browse and search by name or area instead. Photos: the app asks to read your photo library only when you choose to attach a photo to a review or set a profile picture. It uploads only the photo you pick, resized, and never browses your library. Review photos are limited to 5 MB each. You can change these permissions at any time in your phone's Settings. Service providers and where data is stored We use these providers to run the app. They process data on our behalf: Supabase: stores accounts, reviews and photos, and handles sign-in. Our project is hosted in the UK (London region). Apple and Google: distribute the app through the App Store, TestFlight and Google Play. Expo: builds the app. It does not receive your reviews. Maps: the iPhone app uses Apple Maps. If the Android app shows a map, it uses Google Maps. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. How long we keep data and deleting your account We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account in the app under Profile → Delete my account. This permanently removes your profile, reviews, photos, saved places and friendships. Our provider may keep routine backups for a short time afterwards, and they are deleted in line with the provider's normal schedule. If you cannot use the app, email us at jonclark97@hotmail.com from the address on your account and we will delete the account for you. Your rights, children, safety and contact Your rights. Under UK data protection law you can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, and receive a copy. Most of this you can do yourself in the app. For anything else, email jonclark97@hotmail.com. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. Children. Local Grounds is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has signed up, email us and we will delete the account. Safety. You can report any public review and block any person from within the app. We review reports and may remove reviews or accounts that break our rules. Changes. If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app. The date at the top shows when it was last updated. Contact. Jonathan Clark, Local Grounds, Norwich, UK.
